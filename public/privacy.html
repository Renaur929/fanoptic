Privacy Policy

Beta draft · Updated October 28, 2026

This is FanOptic’s beta draft. Add a working contact email and confirm retention and deletion details before public launch.

Information we use

When you create an account, FanOptic stores your name, email address, a password hash (not your plain-text password), chosen teams, and whether you opted in to email updates. FanOptic also records basic engagement events, such as searches and article clicks.

How we use it

We use account information to create and protect your account, verify your email, send account-related messages, and personalize your sports feed. Engagement information is used to understand which teams and features are popular and to improve FanOptic. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers

FanOptic uses Supabase for its account database, Render to host the app, and Resend to deliver verification and account emails. They process information as needed to provide those services.

Email

We send verification and account-related emails. Optional FanOptic updates are sent only when you check the updates box.

Retention and requests

Account data is kept while an account is active. The final retention period and account-deletion process are still being set for this beta. For a request about your data, contact [add FanOptic privacy contact email before public launch].

Third-party links

When you open a linked article, the publisher may receive information through your browser and its own privacy policy applies.
